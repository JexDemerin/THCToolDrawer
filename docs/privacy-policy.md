# Privacy Policy — Together Homecare Chrome Extensions

**Effective date:** August 2026

This page describes the privacy practices for the internal Chrome extensions
built and published by Together Homecare:

- **THC Tool Drawer**
- **THC WellSky Shift Scanner**
- **THC Text Blaster**

These extensions are built for Together Homecare's own staff to use as part of
their jobs. They are not intended for the general public, and installing them
requires being a member of the Together Homecare organization.

---

## What information these extensions handle

**THC Tool Drawer** does not collect or handle any personal information. It
reads a list of internal tool names and links from a private Together Homecare
spreadsheet, and shows them in a panel. It also reads the address, title, and
any text a staff member has selected on the page they are viewing, but only at
the moment they click a button to open a linked tool — this is used to carry
that page's context into the tool being opened, and goes nowhere else.

**THC WellSky Shift Scanner** reads shift records that are already visible to a
staff member inside WellSky (ClearCare) — including caregiver names, client
names, dates, scheduled and actual clock-in/clock-out times, and shift status —
and copies them into a private Together Homecare Google Sheet, so the office can
reconcile scheduled hours against hours actually worked.

**THC Text Blaster** sends text messages through a staff member's own Google
Voice account to a chosen group of caregivers or clients, one at a time, and
records each send — including the phone number and message text — in a private
Together Homecare Google Sheet.

## How this information is used

All information handled by these extensions is used solely for Together
Homecare's internal operations: payroll and scheduling reconciliation, and
caregiver/client communication. None of it is used for advertising, profiling,
or any purpose unrelated to running the business.

## Where this information is stored

Everything these extensions read or write stays inside Together Homecare's own
Google Workspace account — in Google Sheets that Together Homecare owns and
controls. Nothing is sent to the developers of these extensions, and nothing is
stored on any server outside of Google's infrastructure and Together Homecare's
own account.

## Who this information is shared with

Together Homecare does not sell, rent, or share this information with any third
party. It is accessed only by authorized Together Homecare staff for the
purposes described above.

## Data retention

Information is retained in Together Homecare's Google Sheets for as long as it
is needed for scheduling, payroll, and communication records, consistent with
Together Homecare's standard business record-keeping practices.

## Security

Access to the underlying spreadsheets is restricted to authorized staff through
Together Homecare's Google Workspace account. The WellSky Shift Scanner and Text
Blaster extensions operate only within a staff member's own already-authenticated
sessions in WellSky and Google Voice; they do not store separate copies of
account passwords.

## Changes to this policy

If how these extensions handle information changes, this page will be updated.
